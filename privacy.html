Privacy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Playing without signing in

You don't need an account to play. Settings, best scores and saved progress stay in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. It puts your scores on leaderboards and backs up your progress. When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with our sign-in service, Supabase, which keeps them with your account. The games never show them to other players.

For each signed-in player we store:

  • A player name, such as Player-1234, which you can change. It's public on leaderboards.
  • Your scores: the game, the score, the mode or difficulty, the run's seed and the date. These are public on leaderboards.
  • Saved progress for each game. Only you can see it.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.

Games load their code from jsDelivr and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services see your IP address when they send the files.

Deleting your data

To delete your account, scores and saves, open a request on GitHub. Include your player name, but not your email address: the request is public. You can also write to the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen. Data kept only on your device can be removed by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
