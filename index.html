    Gulp!

    A fish-eat-fish game in 3D. Eat smaller fish to grow and dodge the bigger ones across 5 reef levels, from a coral garden to a shipwreck. Keep your size as each level opens into a bigger, wider sea. Find the shard hidden in each level's wreck, grab pearls from snapping oysters, then face the Giant Moray in its lair. All five shards open a portal to the Kelp Forest, with stinging jellyfish and a charging Leopard Shark. Play as a shark, barracuda, orca or swordfish. One bite and the run is over.
